Apex Loop

A full racing arena: pit lane, grandstands, gravel traps and walls. Brake before the corner, steer smoothly, and don’t floor it in first gear — the rear tires will let go. The gearbox is manual: shift up before the redline, and don’t downshift too early or the engine over-revs.

Manual gearbox: E/Q shift up/down (or keys 1–6, N), W gas, S brake, A/D steer, arrow keys move the shifter through the gate (↓ from 1 = N, hold ↓ = 2; ←/→ pick the column), Space handbrake, C camera, V paint, R reset. On a phone, tap a gear in the shifter box.
